Add to Growth Command

One button in your bookmarks bar. While you are looking at a business, tap it and they land on the board with whatever the page already shows. It reads only the page on your screen, fetches nothing, and sends nothing.

  • LinkedIn profile: name, headline, and the company after "at".
  • Instagram profile: handle, display name, bio, and the link in bio.
  • Google Maps listing: business name, website, phone, address, category.
  • Any business website: site name, the first email and phone on the page, and its LinkedIn or Instagram links.

A small window opens with the fields prefilled so you can fix the industry or add a note before saving. If they are already on the board it says so instead of adding a second row.
